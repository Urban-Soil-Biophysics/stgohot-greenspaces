
rasters (.tif, geotiff) = heat surface models at 10m horizontal resolution
CRS: WGS84/local UTM zone (EPSG codes vary)

am = morning (7-8AM); af = afternoon (4-4PM); pm = evening (7-8PM)
t = temperature;
f = Fahrenheit 
c = Celsius
e.g. pm_hi_f.tif = evening heat index geotiff raster with fahrenheit values.
